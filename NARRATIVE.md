# Battles and Barmaids: Enter the Taplands — Narrative Design Document

**Theme:** Culinary Resistance, Wild Fermentation, Anti-Corporate Dystopia
**Tone:** Heartfelt, Quirky, Defiant, and Epic

This game leads up to a larger story, planned as a future game in [docs/story-plans.md](docs/story-plans.md).

## 1. Core Logline
In a world slowly being paved over by a sterile, monolithic Corporate Wall, a barmaid with a mobile kitchen-cart and a party of misfit adventurers fight back with the chaotic magic of wild fermentation, one three-course quest at a time, to hold off the creeping "franchise slop" and reclaim the Taplands.

## 2. The World & Setting
The continent is divided by a literal and ideological frontier. Magic in this world is derived from culinary passion and the living microbiomes of food.

Every region has a tavern, which serves as the barmaid's home base there.

- **The Village of the Hearth:** The prologue setting. A quiet, rustic village anchored by Master Levain's ancient wood-fired stone oven. *Tavern: The Flaming Flagon (Rustic Hearth Cooking).*
- **The Taplands (The Last Frontier):** A vibrant, chaotic region of amber barley fields, copper fermenting vats, and brave roadside inns. It is a place of authentic culture, slow-cooked meals, and community. *Tavern: The Copper Vat Tavern (Wild Fermentation & Brewing).*
- **City of Circuits (Eastern Dunes):** A fallen commercial monolith half-buried in the sand. Defunct neon signs and solid-state pre-decline electronics await salvage by brave foragers. *Tavern: The Neon Skillet (Retro Diner & Salvage Cuisine).*
- **The Crashed Titan Sanctuary (Lake Crater):** A colossal alien war-machine fallen into a lake. Its flooded crater serves as the ultimate future home base for the culinary resistance. *Tavern: The Geothermal Galley (Deep-Crater Feasting).*
- **The Corporate Wall (The Monolith):** A creeping, grey-and-neon border of moving mega-fortresses. The Wall slowly rolls westward, ingesting boutique shops, independent farms, and taverns, only to extrude uniform, sterile franchise chains and endless vats of "pink slime." *Tavern: The Liberated Cafeteria (Reclaimed Franchise Slop).*

Only the Village of the Hearth has quests so far.

## 3. Factions & Magic Systems

**The Artisan Resistance (The "Wild" Magic)**
Magic here is known as Zest. It is chaotic, deeply flavorful, and alive. It is channeled through The Living Sourdough Starter — a symbiotic organism that must be fed (with stoneground rye, wellspring water, or wild spores) and tended to. A healthy starter radiates vitality, blessing the frontline fighters with physical resilience and morale.

**The Franchise Syndicate (The "Slop" Magic)**
The Syndicate utilizes algorithmic efficiency, artificial preservatives, and mass-produced uniformity. Their foot soldiers are literal manifestations of fast-food byproducts (Pink Slime Mutants, Deep-Fried Golems). Their magic suppresses individuality, numbing the mind and standardizing the soul.

## 4. Characters

### The Barmaids (playable)
The player picks one barmaid to run the mobile kitchen-cart. She doesn't fight directly. Each round she serves ingredients to her patrons or tosses them at the monster.

- **Marigold the Mender:** Heavy healing, light offense. Tends wounds, ladles out a Round of Comfort for the whole party, and tosses bitter herbs to dull the enemy's attack. Her special, **Last Call Miracle**, fully heals and fortifies the weakest patron.
- **Saffron the Morale-Brewer:** Big buffs, a splash of healing. Pours flaming shots that let a patron's next strike cut through armor, Rally Cry Ales that permanently raise attack, and Chicory Cures. Her special, **Tavern Anthem**, lets the whole party's next strikes cut through armor.
- **Bunhilda the Brawler:** Direct damage and debuffs, with just enough healing to get by. Hurls Flaming Shots to the Face, haunts monsters with Ghost Pepperoni Slices, and patches patrons up with Bar Rag Bandages. Her special, **Holy Ham Grenade**, does massive damage.

### The Brigade (patrons)
Five adventurers wait at the bar. The barmaid drafts three for each quest.

- **Princess Blue (Natural Leaven Healer, Paladin):** The true, gentle personality of a King's daughter, still living in the palace under the Syndicate's corporate "pink-slime" mind control. When free of the programming, she sneaks out to heal the resistance. The sturdiest patron and the one monsters target most. Her **Blessed Stew** heals the weakest ally.
- **Grimble the Griddle-Master (Warrior):** A hard-hitting brawler with a griddle and a hammer. His **Meat-Hook Smash** deals double damage.
- **Willow (Village Pine Scout, Ranger):** A childhood companion from the Village of the Hearth. An agile archer who knows every game trail and salt deposit in the Taplands ridges. Her **Skewering Toothpick** wounds the monster and saps its attack.
- **Faelen the Fermenter (Rogue):** A fragile but deadly fighter who carries a fermentation jar. Their **Zesty Backstab** carves past armor for heavy damage.
- **Unit Micro-90 (Vending T-800, Mage):** A defected Syndicate vending bot, sent back from a bleak processed-food future. Originally designed to dispense flavorless nutrient paste, it gained sentience after a drop of wild sourdough starter fell into its circuitry. Reprogrammed to fight corporate oppression by weaponizing molten convenience food. The hardest hitter and the most fragile. Its **Liquid Nitrogen Blast** damages and slows the monster.

## 5. How the World Plays

- **Taverns are home base.** Between quests, the barmaid stocks her pantry at the market and picks the night's quest from the specials board.
- **Quests are three-course dinners.** Each quest serves three monsters in a row, one per course, ending with the boss. Patrons get a quick healing snack between courses.
- **Ingredients are the barmaid's ammunition:** Mighty Malt, Aged Cheddar, Chilly Peppers, Watery Onion, Holy Ham and Ghost Pepperoni. Every ability costs some, and quests drop more. Polishing tumblers skips her turn to scavenge one.
- **Zest builds as the fight goes on.** The barmaid's Super Charge fills as she serves, unlocking her special at 100%. Each patron's Appetite Meter fills as they attack and take hits, triggering their special when full.
- **Gear matters.** Patrons are re-equipped with random weapons and armor at each draft. Weapons carry a damage type (fillet, skewer, tenderize, retro) or an element (spicy, frosty, tingly, umami), and some monsters resist one, which halves the damage.
- **Winning pays.** Clearing a quest earns 30–60 gold in tips plus ingredient drops. Clearing a region's boss opens the road to the next region. A failed quest costs 15 gold.

## 6. Chapter 1: The Village of the Hearth

The Corporate Wall is still a distant rumor, but local wildlife is becoming corrupted by synthetic runoff. The barmaid's first nights at The Flaming Flagon are spent clearing out what's crawled into the village:

- **Mystery Meat! Clearing the Cellar Rats:** Lil Nibbler, Ratta Rogue, and Ratta Chewie, the Cellar King. Ratta Chewie is already "grown fat on the stew," too tender to be tenderized further.
- **Goblin Raid on the Larder:** Little Gobbler, Greener Goblin, and Gobla Cado Grande, the largest guac-goblin the Larder has seen.
- **The Bored King (Boss):** Braised Bandit, Mutton Chopper, and The Baconnator, a boar built of bacon, the village's Great Boar. He's "sizzling and vast," so frosty attacks barely register.

Defeating The Baconnator opens the road to the Taplands.
